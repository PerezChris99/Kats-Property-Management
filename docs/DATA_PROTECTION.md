# Uganda data protection request workflow — pending operational acceptance

Kats now supports ACCESS, RECTIFY, ERASURE, RESTRICT and OBJECT requests from
authenticated users. The requester can see only their own cases; the
business owner/super-admin can triage and record the decision. A basic
authenticated personal-data summary is available. Closing an erasure
request requires a recorded check of financial/audit retention exceptions.

**No case automatically destroys tenant, financial, tax or immutable audit
records.** Operators must verify the identity and legal basis, collect any
documents needed, send an adequate response through approved secure channels
and record completion evidence. This is a request-management workflow, not a
fully automated DSAR export/deletion engine.

Before launch: verify registration/renewal with the Ugandan PDPO where
required, controller/processor contracts, lawful bases, privacy notices,
cross-border processing safeguards, local and cloud data locations, retention
schedule and breach notification. Work with qualified Uganda privacy counsel
for statutory response deadlines and exceptions.

Sources: Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, Regulations 2021,
PDPO official guidance. Internal correctness does not confer registration.
