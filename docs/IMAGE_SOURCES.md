# Ugandan imagery used on the KATS public website

All five images are **real photographs of residential buildings or
neighborhoods in Uganda**. They are published by their contributors on
Wikimedia Commons under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
(CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Each remains owned by its original creator. KATS uses CSS cropping to fit
hero and page components; cropping is a presentation change and reuse is
subject to the licence, including ShareAlike conditions for adapted material.
The public footer exposes authors, exact source pages and the licence.
No building or photographer is presented as a KATS client or partner.

`frontend/src/site/media.js`. The images are linked directly as fixed 1280px
Wikimedia thumbnails, not scraped from a random image search endpoint. The
hero also respects users who have requested reduced animation.
